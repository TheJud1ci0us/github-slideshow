LingoPod — Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026.

What stays on your phone

Speech recognition, translation, your saved words and sentences, your shadowing recordings and your statistics are processed and stored only on your device. Recordings made with the microphone (shadowing) are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

What goes over the internet

  • Podcasts: episodes and artwork are downloaded from the podcasts' own public servers; the podcast directory search uses Apple's public search service. These services see your IP address, as any web request does.
  • Models: speech and translation models are downloaded from Hugging Face and Google when you ask for them.
  • Purchases (LingoPod Pro): to confirm a purchase, the app sends its Google Play purchase token, a Google Play Integrity token and a random app-generated identifier to our purchase-check service, which asks Google and replies with a signed pass. Nothing is stored by this service.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (optional): only if you agree at first launch (and changeable in Profile → Privacy), anonymous counts such as which screens are used are sent to TelemetryDeck, with no personal data.

No ads, no selling

LingoPod shows no advertising and does not sell or share personal data.

Children

LingoPod is not directed at children under 13.
