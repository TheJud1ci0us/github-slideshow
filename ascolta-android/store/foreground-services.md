# Play Console → App content → Foreground service permissions

**FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK** — *Media playback*
> LingoPod is a podcast player. The service keeps an episode playing with the screen off and shows
> the media notification with play/pause.
Video: start an episode, lock the phone, show the notification controls.

**FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC** — *Data transfer / sync (user-initiated downloads)*
> Downloads podcast episodes and speech-recognition models the user chose to download; shows
> progress in a notification and can be cancelled.
Video: tap Download on an episode, show the progress notification.

**FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROCESSING** — *Media processing*
> Transcribes the audio of an episode on the device (speech recognition), started by the user
> with "Transcribe"; shows progress in a notification and can be stopped.
Video: tap Transcribe on an episode, show the transcription notification and the text appearing.

Record each 10–20 s video with the phone's screen recorder and upload it (YouTube unlisted link).
