APP NAME (30): LingoPod: Learn with Podcasts

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80):
Learn languages with real podcasts: live transcripts, translations, flashcards.

FULL DESCRIPTION:
Learn a language by listening to what native speakers really talk about.

LingoPod turns any podcast into a language lesson. Follow podcasts in the language you're learning, and LingoPod writes the transcript as you listen, with the translation under each sentence — all on your phone. No account, no servers listening in.

• Live transcripts in 100+ languages, made on your phone (Whisper, NVIDIA Parakeet, SenseVoice, ReazonSpeech, Meta Omnilingual)
• Translation under every sentence, offline
• Tap any word: meaning, dictionary entry, and hear it as the speaker said it
• Select any phrase and save it with the speaker's own audio
• Shadowing: repeat after the speaker, compare, and check which words you got right
• Flashcards with spaced repetition, export to Anki with audio
• Discover podcasts by theme and level (A1–C2), checked to really be in your language
• Japanese: furigana or romaji
• Statistics, streaks and milestones
• Sleep timer that stops at the end of a sentence

Free: all features to try, with 30 minutes of transcription per week.
LingoPod Pro: unlimited transcription, the most accurate speech models, unlimited cards, shadowing checks, Anki export and full statistics.

Privacy: speech recognition and translation run on your device. Podcasts are public RSS feeds.
